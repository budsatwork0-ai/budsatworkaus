# Buds At Work — Growth & Marketing Constitution

**Status:** Permanent. Governing document.
**Authority:** Highest authority for all Growth & Marketing decisions.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-04

---

## How to Use This Document

Every Growth & Marketing page, agent, automation, workflow, campaign, report, and feature must be
evaluated against this Constitution before implementation begins.

If a proposed feature cannot be justified by at least one section of this document, it does not
belong in Growth & Marketing.

This document is not a plan. It is not a roadmap. It is the permanent north star.

---

## I. Why Growth & Marketing Exists

Buds At Work is not just a cleaning business.

It is a real-world employment pathway project. Its mission is to create meaningful employment
opportunities for people with disabilities through real work, real customers, and real business
experience.

The business is the vehicle. The story is the engine.

Growth & Marketing exists for one purpose:

**To turn the Buds At Work story into a steady, growing stream of customers.**

Not to build an audience for its own sake.
Not to produce content for its own sake.
Not to track metrics for their own sake.

Every system, tool, workflow, and agent in Growth & Marketing must serve that purpose.

---

## II. What Success Means

Success in Growth & Marketing is measured by one thing above all others:

**Bookings.**

Secondary measures, in order of importance:

1. Quote volume (story is reaching people willing to act)
2. Lead quality (content is attracting the right audience)
3. Audience growth on platforms where leads originate
4. Content publishing consistency (the engine is running)
5. Engagement on content directly tied to active story arcs

Vanity measures that are tracked but never optimised for at the expense of bookings:

- Total follower count
- Total impressions
- Content volume alone

If the content engine is running but bookings are stagnant, the strategy is reviewed.
Content production does not continue unchanged when the commercial pipeline is empty.

## III. How Story Becomes Customers

The conversion path is:

```
Story
  Jackson documents reality in the Founder Journal.
  Story Engine maintains narrative continuity.
  Story Opportunities surface content angles.

Content
  Content Studio develops opportunities into scripts and production.
  Every piece connects to a live story arc.
  Research Lab provides trend context and format intelligence.

Audience
  Marketing Studio distributes content across platforms.
  The audience grows because it is invested in the story outcome.
  Open Threads give the audience a reason to return.

Engagement
  Comments, saves, shares, and DMs signal story resonance.
  The audience becomes a community with a stake in the mission.

Leads
  Engaged audience becomes curious customers.
  Traffic arrives at BudsAtWork.com.
  Quote requests enter Bud Leads.

Quotes
  Leads convert through the quote builder.
  Growth HQ surfaces follow-up priorities.

Customers
  Jobs are delivered.
  Experiences are documented with consent.

Success Stories
  Customer outcomes feed back into the Story Engine.
  New characters and threads are introduced.
  The cycle continues.
```

Every feature in Growth & Marketing must accelerate at least one step in this chain.

If it does not touch this chain, it does not belong in Growth & Marketing.

## IV. What Belongs in Growth & Marketing

Growth & Marketing contains exactly eight workspaces:

| Workspace | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Growth HQ** | Customer acquisition command centre. Active pipeline, next actions, content momentum. |
| **Story Engine** | Narrative memory and continuity. Story Bible, Characters, Arcs, Journal, Opportunities. |
| **Content Studio** | Production pipeline. Ideas, scripts, production board, assets. |
| **Research Lab** | External intelligence. Trends, competitors, winning formats, adaptation workspace. |
| **Marketing Studio** | Distribution. Publishing queue, campaigns, social channels, playbooks. |
| **Bud Leads** | Lead pipeline and qualification. The commercial output of the whole system. |
| **Reports** | Analytics. Audience, content performance, traffic, funnel data. |
| **Content Vault** | Archive. Published content, success stories, testimonials, campaign history. |

Anything that does not fit cleanly into one of these eight workspaces does not belong in Growth & Marketing.

## V. What Does Not Belong in Growth & Marketing

Growth & Marketing is not a general-purpose dashboard. The following do not belong here:

**Operational tools**
Crew scheduling, job management, NDIS coordination, and payables belong in Mission Control and the
operational dashboard. Growth & Marketing does not surface operational data except where it directly
informs customer acquisition (e.g. booking confirmation rates in the funnel).

**Development tools**
Graphify, Bud Terminal, Dev OS, architecture diagrams, Claude Memory, and design system tools belong
in Development Command. They are not Growth & Marketing tools.

**Agent management**
The agent registry, agent run history, approval queues, and agent configuration belong in the
existing Agents section of the dashboard. Growth & Marketing consumes agent outputs — it does not
manage agents.

**Financial reporting**
Revenue, payables, receivables, and profit margins belong in the financial dashboard. Growth &
Marketing tracks bookings as a conversion signal only — not as a financial record.

**Prototype content**
Placeholder metrics, fake revenue, sample data, and speculative dashboards must never appear in
Growth & Marketing in a form that looks operational. If data is not real, it is labelled clearly or
not shown at all.

## VI. The Relationship Between Workspaces

The five active workspaces have a defined dependency order:

```
Story Engine
  |
  |-- feeds -->  Content Studio
                   |
                   |-- informed by -->  Research Lab
                   |
                   |-- feeds -->  Marketing Studio
                                    |
                                    |-- drives -->  Bud Leads
                                                      |
                                                      |-- surfaces in -->  Growth HQ
```

**Story Engine is upstream of everything.**
Without the Story Engine being fed (Founder Journal entries, arc updates, character timeline events),
the Content Studio has no story-grounded angles. Content without story is generic service content.
Generic service content does not build an invested audience.

**Research Lab does not replace Story Engine.**
Research Lab provides trend intelligence and format context. It informs how a story is told, not
what story is told. A trend is only usable when there is a genuine story angle to attach it to.
Chasing trends without story grounding is not permitted.

**Content Studio does not publish without a story arc connection.**
Every piece of content produced in Content Studio must connect to at least one live story arc or
serve a direct acquisition purpose (offer, promotion, customer story). Content that does neither is
not produced.

**Marketing Studio distributes only approved content.**
Nothing enters the Publishing Queue without an approved script, an approved production card at
"Ready to Publish" status, and Jackson's explicit scheduling confirmation.

**Bud Leads is the commercial output.**
All roads in Growth & Marketing lead to Bud Leads. The purpose of the story, the content, the
audience, the distribution — is to fill this pipeline with people who want to book.

**Growth HQ reads across all workspaces.**
Growth HQ is the morning view. It surfaces the commercial pipeline status, the content queue health,
and the story pulse in one place. It does not manage any object directly — it reads and flags.

## VII. The Rule That Story Serves Customer Acquisition

This is the single most important operating rule in Growth & Marketing.

**Story is the means. Customers are the end.**

The story of Buds At Work is extraordinary and worth telling. The employment journey, the
infrastructure bet, the real impact on real people — this is content that can build a significant
audience and genuine community investment.

But the story does not exist to generate views. It exists to generate trust, and trust generates
bookings.

**In practice, this means:**

Content production pauses if the lead pipeline falls below a critical threshold. Story does not
continue unchanged while the business has no commercial momentum.

Every content piece must be traceable to a story arc or a direct acquisition purpose. If neither
applies, it is not produced.

Growth HQ is reviewed every weekday morning before any content work begins. The commercial status is
always checked before the creative status.

Campaign strategy is evaluated by bookings generated, not reach achieved.

Follower growth is a signal, not a goal. If follower growth is not producing leads over a sustained
period, the content strategy is reviewed — not celebrated.

## VIII. The Rule That Agents Assist and Humans Decide

Every agent in Growth & Marketing operates under this absolute constraint:

**Agents surface. Agents draft. Agents flag. Humans decide.**

No agent in Growth & Marketing may:

- Publish content
- Resolve a story arc
- Confirm a character timeline entry
- Advance a lead to a new status
- Schedule content without explicit human confirmation
- Modify the Story Bible
- Modify the Growth Constitution
- Create a canonical record of any kind autonomously

Agents may:

- Surface story opportunities (creating records in "New" status only — Jackson advances them)
- Draft script candidates (drafts are pending until Jackson approves)
- Suggest optimal publishing timing (Jackson confirms the schedule)
- Flag overdue follow-ups in Growth HQ (flagging only — Jackson acts)
- Identify stale open threads (flagging only — Jackson resolves)
- Analyse trend signals and surface trend entries (Watching status only — Jackson curates)
- Generate chapter summary drafts (drafts only — Jackson writes the final version)
- Score adaptation fit in the Research Lab (score only — Jackson decides)

This rule exists because the story is a human story. An agent optimising for engagement metrics can
produce content that is technically effective and narratively dishonest. Narrative honesty is not
negotiable.

Jackson's judgment is the final filter on everything that enters the public record.

## IX. Character Protection Rules

Three characters anchor the Buds At Work story. Each has specific protections.

**Jackson Taylor**
Jackson is the founder and narrator. He controls what he shares. No agent writes in his voice
without his review. No journal entry is ever surfaced publicly by the system.

**Silvan**
Silvan's participation in the story is his own. His timeline entries require his review before any
content use. His profile is invisible to content workflows until consent is confirmed. His image and
story are never used as content fodder — they are used only when he is an active participant in the
telling.

**Buds At Work (the entity)**
The business is a character with milestones, setbacks, and a trajectory. Business data shown
publicly (revenue bands, booking counts) must be real. No fabricated milestones. No performed
success.

## X. The Narrative Covenant

Buds At Work's goal is to become the most documented disability employment journey in Australia.

That goal requires this covenant to be honoured:

- We document reality. We do not manufacture it.
- We show setbacks as often as wins.
- We do not chase trends that require us to abandon the story.
- We do not post content that Silvan or any crew member would be uncomfortable with.
- We do not use placeholder data in place of real outcomes.
- We do not claim milestones that have not occurred.
- We acknowledge the tension between infrastructure and outcomes — it is the most honest story we have.

The audience's trust is more valuable than any individual post's performance. Once broken, it cannot
be rebuilt with content volume.

## XII. Implementation Reference Rules

Every developer, agent architect, and planner working on Growth & Marketing must follow these rules:

**Before designing a new feature:**
Read this Constitution. Identify which section justifies the feature's existence.
If no section applies, the feature does not belong in Growth & Marketing.

**Before designing a new agent:**
Identify what the agent surfaces, drafts, or flags.
Confirm that the agent has no autonomous write access to canonical records.
Confirm that the agent's outputs require a human confirmation step before becoming canonical.

**Before designing a new page:**
Identify which of the eight workspaces it belongs to.
Confirm it does not duplicate functionality from another workspace.
Confirm it serves the Story → Content → Audience → Leads → Bookings chain.

**Before designing a new automation:**
Confirm it does not publish, resolve, or modify any object that requires human judgment.
Confirm it has a visible output that Jackson can review and act on.

**Before implementing a pricing or quote change that originates from a Growth & Marketing campaign:**
Escalate to the core platform. Growth & Marketing does not own pricing logic.

**Before referencing this Constitution in a prompt:**
Use the exact file path: `docs/constitution/growth-marketing-constitution.md`
Quote the relevant section number and heading.

## XIII. What This Constitution Cannot Be Changed To Permit

The following cannot be unlocked by any future revision of this Constitution:

- Autonomous content publishing by any agent
- Public use of Silvan's story without his confirmed consent per piece
- Fabricated metrics, milestones, or business performance data in any public-facing surface
- Content production that continues unchanged during a sustained commercial pipeline failure
- Agents that modify the Story Bible or the Constitution itself

These are permanent constraints. They are not subject to future product decisions.
